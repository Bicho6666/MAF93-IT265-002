# Nightfall
## Genre: horror,  survival 
## Materials: D4, D6, D8, D10 dice, cards

## Summary 
You are  on a 7 night camping trip with friends. You roll D6 based on what starter pack you get for your camping trip. 1 being the worst with only a flashlight and sleeping bag, 6  being a fire log, knife, flashlight, sleeping bag, snacks. You have a health bar, each round you get to roll again for one additional thing to add to your pack out of 6 decks. You will need these items especially at night. At  night a creature comes out (Wendigo) and you have to use your items to either attack or heal yourself. The game goes as long as you survive or kill the monster. There are times where you can help or betray friends but risk other rounds. 

Fire needs at least 3 logs to stay alive
 if not enough logs monster attacks 2x damage
Fire loses 1 every round
Player and monster starts with 100% health
Monster and player rolls D8 for damage
Player can only role if you have weapon
Every time has x amount of times you can use it until it breaks
As more rounds go on the monster attacks heavier, deals more damage [0.5x more damage 2nd round, 1x more damage 3rd round and up]
You have to decide and make good decisions in what items to use in order to survive 
You can give away one card to another player your choosing/decision each round

## **Items:**
Sleeping bag → allows you to skip the night (1x), no attack/damage round

Flashlight → blinds monster and you take half damage or none (2x)

Fire logs → adds to fire life (fire needs at least 3 logs to stay alive)

Knife → uses D8 damage (3x)

Stick → uses D4 damage (1x)

Gun → uses D10 damage (5x) 

Snacks → heals you candy bar 10%, pizza  20%, hobo meal  30%, hamburger 35%

Sacrifice card → any damage dealt to you can be transferred to another player

First Aid card → heals you 50%

Swap → swap deck with another player

Revive → can revive any dead players

## **Game mechanics:**
Starter Pack System (D6 Roll)
Your opening roll determines your initial survival odds:
- 1 = worst loadout (flashlight + sleeping bag)
- 6 = best loadout (fire log, knife, flashlight, sleeping bag, snacks)
## Item Acquisition
Each round, players draw from one of six item decks, gaining:
- Weapons
- Healing items
- Fire logs
- Utility cards
- Social cards (swap, sacrifice, revive)
## **Fire Management**
Fire is a core survival mechanic:
- Needs 3 logs to stay active.
- Loses 1 log per round.
- If fire is below 3 logs:
- Wendigo deals 2× damage that round.

## **Combat System**
- Both player and monster start at 100% health.
- Both roll D8 for base damage.
- Players can only attack if they have a weapon.
Weapons have limited uses:
- Knife (3 uses, D8)
- Stick (1 use, D4)
- Gun (5 uses, D10)
## Damage escalates:
- Round 1: normal
- Round 2: monster deals 1.5× damage
- Round 3+: monster deals 2× damage

## **Night Phase**
At night:
- The Wendigo attacks.
Players choose to:
- Attack
- Heal
- Use defensive items
- Skip the night (sleeping bag, 1 use)
- Blind the monster (flashlight, 2 uses)

## **Social Mechanics**
Players can:
- Give 1 card to another player each round.
- Use Sacrifice to redirect damage to another player.
- Use Swap to exchange decks.
- Use Revive to bring back a dead player.
→ These mechanics create:
- Alliances
- Betrayals
- Negotiation
- Revenge arcs

## **Healing System**
Snacks heal small amounts:
Candy bar: 10%
Pizza: 20%
Hobo meal: 30%
Hamburger: 35%
Major healing:
First Aid: 50%
Healing becomes a strategic choice:
- Heal now and survive?
- Or save it for later when damage spikes?

## **Escalating Difficulty Curve**
The Wendigo becomes more dangerous each night:
- Night 1–2: manageable
- Night 3–4: threatening
- Night 5–6: deadly
- Night 7: final showdown
This curve forces players to:
- Save strong weapons (Gun, Knife) for later nights.
- Use weaker items early (Stick, small snacks).
- Build fire early to avoid late‑game double damage.

## **Item Economy Over Seven Nights**
Players must think long‑term:
- Fire logs must be collected steadily or the fire will die before Night 7.
- Weapons have limited uses, so players must plan when to attack.
- Healing becomes more valuable as damage spikes.
- Utility cards (Swap, Sacrifice, Revive) become political tools.

## **Win/Loss Conditions**
**Win by:**
- Killing the Wendigo before Night 7 ends.
- Surviving all 7 nights with at least 1 HP.
- Being the last living player (competitive mode).

**Lose by:**
- Dying before Night 7.
- Fire going out and taking lethal double damage.
- Running out of weapons and being unable to fight.
- Being sacrificed or betrayed by another player.
